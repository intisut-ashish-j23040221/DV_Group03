Advanced D3 Data Explorations

Showcasing unique ways to visualize Australian enforcement data

State Enforcement Radar

Compare the "DNA" of different jurisdictions. This radar chart plots states across 5 dimensions. To avoid confusion, **select a state below** to see its unique profile, or compare multiple states by selecting them.

Historical Safety Ranking

This "Bump Chart" shows how states rank against each other based on their **Breath Test Safety** (lowest positive rate). A rank of 1 means the state had the lowest percentage of positive tests that year. Watch how states trade places over 15 years.

Detection to Outcome Flow

This **Chord Diagram** visualizes the relationship between how an infringement is detected and its ultimate legal result. The thickness of the ribbons represents the volume of cases. Notice how Cameras (Red/Pink) dominate financial fines, while Police-issued stops (Blue) lead to the majority of Arrests and Charges.

State Enforcement Choropleth

A geographical view of total fines across Australia. Darker shades indicate higher total enforcement volume. Hover over a state to see its core statistics.
